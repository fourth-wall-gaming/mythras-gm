# New to Mythras?

Read this out to a first-time player, in your own words, in about a minute. Do
not paste it at them wholesale, and do not turn it into a rules lecture — the
whole point of the next paragraph is that they do not have to learn any of it.

---

## You don't need to know the rules

You are playing with a Gamesmaster who owns the rulebook, rolls the dice, and
does the arithmetic. You say what your character does; the engine works out
whether it happened. Nobody will ask you to look up a table.

So the only reason to know anything about Mythras going in is that it is a
genuinely unusual system and knowing *three* things about it will make the game
read better.

## The three things worth knowing

**1. Skills are percentages, and you already have them.** There is no d20, no
"roll high and add a modifier". Your character has, say, Athletics 71 — so you
roll d100 and want 71 or under. That's it. It means you always know roughly how
good you are at something, and a 71 feels different from a 34 in a way that is
immediately obvious at the table.

**2. Combat is about *where* and *what shape*, not just how much.** Mythras rolls
hit locations — an arm, the abdomen, the head — and armour is worn per location.
And when you win an exchange you often get to choose a **Special Effect**: impale
the weapon so it sticks, bypass their armour, trip them, take their weapon away.
Fights are short, tactical and frightening, and a single bad exchange can end a
limb. People do not trade blows for ten rounds in this game.

**3. Passions are the good bit.** Alongside skills, characters have Passions —
*Loyalty (my captain) 60*, *Hate (being taken for dumb muscle) 75*. They are
rated like skills, they can be rolled, and they can push a skill roll when
they're driving what you're doing. They are also the fastest way to make a
character feel like a person rather than a stat block, so when you make one,
spend real thought there.

That is the whole briefing. Everything else you will pick up by playing.

## The rules, if you want them

This game runs **Classic Fantasy Imperative** — the fantasy-with-classes flavour
of Mythras. Both Imperative rulebooks are published by The Design Mechanism
under the ORC licence, and both are **free**.

- **[Classic Fantasy Imperative](https://thedesignmechanism.com/classic-fantasy-imperative-pdf/)**
  — what this game actually runs. Classes, spell lists, the lot.
- **[Mythras Imperative](https://thedesignmechanism.com/mythras-imperative/)** —
  the core system on its own. Also on
  [DriveThruRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/185299/mythras-imperative)
  and [itch.io](https://design-mechanism.itch.io/mythras-imperative).
- **[The Design Mechanism](https://thedesignmechanism.com/)** — the publisher,
  and the full Mythras line if the Imperative version sells you on it.

## How to use this at the table

Mention it **once**, when a player is new, before they make a character — because
knowing that Passions matter changes what they write down. Offer the links, do
not insist on them, and then get on with the game.

Never quote rules text at a player mid-scene. The mechanics stay behind the
screen; see `TABLE.md §3`.
