_Concentration, Touch._ Reaches past whoever is wearing the body and speaks to what is riding inside it. The host hears both halves and cannot prevent it; a seated spirit may refuse to answer but cannot pretend not to have heard.

Demonologists use it to give instructions. Woodland practitioners use it to ask a captive what was done to it, which is generally the first honest conversation such a thing has had.

**Bindings that rest on torment are vulnerable here.** A spirit held by an unbearable memory can sometimes be reached simply by somebody willing to listen to it.
