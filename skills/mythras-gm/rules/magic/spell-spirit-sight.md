_Concentration._ The caster perceives spirits directly — free ones, bound ones, and the workings that hold them. A living person carrying a passenger reads as a lamp behind a coat: the shape is right and the light is not the same colour as the rest.

Also shows **seats** (drawn or chalked), **anchors**, and whether a working is still live. It does not read thoughts, does not identify a spirit by name, and cannot see through a hand thick of earth or stone.

Both schools have this and it is the only spell most woodland practitioners ever want.
