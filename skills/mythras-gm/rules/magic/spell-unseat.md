_Concentration, Touch._ The counter, and the reason the old ways bothered to learn any of this.

Unseat does not extract and does not capture. It **breaks the seat** — undoes the working that holds a passenger in a vessel — and what is bound goes where such things go when nothing is holding them.

- The host is left empty, alive, and unharmed by the unseating itself.
- Any **channel** still open closes as the seat breaks, and can then be healed normally.
- It requires **contact with the vessel**, so somebody must be willing to put their hands on a possessed person and keep them there.

**It cannot be forced on a spirit that is holding on.** A passenger seated against its will is freed easily; one that wants to stay must be dealt with some other way, and a spirit held by torment counts as *wanting to stay* until somebody addresses the torment. See **Speak with the Bound**.

Practitioners taught by the old ways in a place where people died badly tend to learn this first. It is not considered a combat spell. It is considered a burial rite.
