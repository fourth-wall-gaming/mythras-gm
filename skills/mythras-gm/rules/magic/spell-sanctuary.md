_Concentration._ Denies entry. While concentrating, the caster refuses any unseated spirit passage into a warded person, doorway or space — including a spirit that has just been drawn forth and is looking for somewhere to be.

It does nothing to a spirit already seated, and it does not harm anything. It simply will not let it in.

The standard woodland use is to stand over somebody having a binding taken off them, so that whatever comes out cannot go straight into the person doing the work.
