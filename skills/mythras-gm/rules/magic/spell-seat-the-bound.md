_Concentration, Touch, Resist Willpower._ The binding proper, and the hard one.

**Requires both:** a vessel with an **Open Channel** already cut, and a **seat** drawn on the ground — a worked figure with corners, which is not a circle. A circle is protective and goes round the caster; a seat goes round the subject and holds a thing in place while it is put somewhere.

On success the spirit is seated, the channel closes, and the vessel is thereafter a host. Resisted by the *spirit* with Willpower, not by the vessel — a proud or honourable spirit resists hardest, and a betrayed one hardest of all.

**On failure the seat is spent and the channel remains open.** This is the most common way an amateur ruins a subject: the mark is made, the seat is drawn, the working slides off, and the caster is left with a prepared vessel, no passenger, and a woman who will not stop bleeding.

Leaving the figure is dangerous. A drawn seat is still live for as long as it is unswept, and it will go on holding the channel open from across a room, through a wall, for as long as anybody leaves it on the floor.
