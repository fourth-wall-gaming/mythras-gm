_Concentration, Touch, Resist Willpower._ Lifts a seated spirit out of a living host **intact**, which is the difficult part. Any fool with a knife can put a thing into somebody; getting one out whole and putting it somewhere better is the work of a lifetime.

**A drawn spirit must have somewhere to be.** It cannot be held in the open. If there is no prepared vessel and no anchor in reach at the moment of extraction, it goes into the nearest living thing standing over it, which is normally the caster.

The vacated host survives and is thereafter empty and his own — which, if the seating was done in childhood, means a body that has grown around a passenger and a person who has never used it alone.

Requires an anchor for the spirit in question. Without one, the extraction is Formidable at best.
