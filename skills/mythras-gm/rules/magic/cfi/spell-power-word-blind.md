_Conjuration_

**Rank:** Mage 4 · **Casting Time:** 1 Action · **Sphere:** None · **Duration:** See Below · **Cost:** 3, +1/additional Intensity · **Range:** 20 ft/Intensity · **Area:** 15 ft. Radius · **Resist:** Endurance

Upon successfully casting this spell, one creature of the caster's choice within both line of sight and Range, is struck blind, along with all others in a 15 ft. radius that fail a Resistance roll. This blindness may be cured by the casting of Cure Blindness or Dispel Magic. The spell's Duration is modified by the target's Rank or SIZ, whichever gives the more beneficial result.
