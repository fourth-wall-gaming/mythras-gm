**This page is a signpost. Do not rule from it.**

The binding school used to be nine spells in this graph — Spirit Sight, Speak
with the Bound, Open the Channel, Seat the Bound, Reinforce the Seat, Anchor,
Draw Forth, Unseat, Sanctuary — cast on a Magic skill for one Magic Point each.

They were never spells. None of them is one Action, one Cost and one target; the
sequence runs over days, needs physical objects, and has to be reinforced every
morning. Written as spells they kept drifting, because the format was fighting
the thing.

They are now **powers** on the Mythras chassis, which is also what makes them
publishable — see `magic/powers/how-powers-work-here`.

- The demonologists' sequence: **`magic/powers/the-binding`**
- The wild-elf counter-set: **`magic/powers/the-wild-line`**
