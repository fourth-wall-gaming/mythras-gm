Not a separate magic system. These are ordinary spells in the **Magic** framework
(`magic/casting`) — Magic skill, one Magic Point, Intensity 1 — that happen to be **taught
nowhere**. They are not in libraries and they are not sold. A practitioner has them because
somebody who had them decided to pass them on, which is why they run in families and cults and
in one or two traditions that would be appalled to be mentioned in the same paragraph.

## The demonologists' set

`spell-spirit-sight` · `spell-speak-with-the-bound` · `spell-open-the-channel` ·
`spell-seat-the-bound` · `spell-reinforce-the-seat` · `spell-anchor` · `spell-draw-forth`

A complete binding is a sequence and every step is a separate casting:

1. **Open the Channel** — cut a vessel that will not close.
2. Draw a **seat** — the worked figure with corners. Not a spell; a physical form, and it can be
   copied by somebody who cannot cast at all.
3. **Seat the Bound** — put the spirit in. The channel closes as it seats.
4. **Anchor** — one or two objects that hold a piece of the spirit, making everything afterwards
   easier.
5. **Reinforce the Seat** — every morning, for as long as the host lasts.
6. **Draw Forth** — when the host is used up, and only with an anchor and somewhere to put it.

**Each step is a separate point of failure and the party can attack any of them.** Take the
anchors and the rite gets hard. Sweep the seat and an unfinished channel closes. Reach the
spirit and the whole thing may come apart from inside.

## The woodland set

`spell-spirit-sight` · `spell-speak-with-the-bound` · `spell-unseat` · `spell-sanctuary`

The old ways learned the first two by necessity and the last two on purpose. There is no
offensive spell in the set and there never was: **Unseat is a burial rite**, performed by putting
your hands on somebody and refusing to let go until what is riding them can leave.

The two schools share Spirit Sight and Speak with the Bound and nothing else, and each regards
the other's remaining spells as obscene.
