_Touch._ A binding wears. The spirit pulls, the vessel ages, and the seat loosens — so it is re-tightened, and the standard practice is once a day at first light.

The rite is performed on the same place every morning, on the original channel, which is reopened and bled. **The bleeding is the mechanism, not cruelty**: it draws off what has worn through, and leaves a grey residue round the wound that is the spirit's wear coming out of the host.

A second effect that keepers rely on and rarely write down: **it leaves the passenger tired.** A spirit that has just been reinforced argues less. Skipping the rite for a day makes the passenger louder and the host more himself.

Made easier by holding an **Anchor** for the same spirit. Holding two makes it routine; holding none makes it very hard indeed.
