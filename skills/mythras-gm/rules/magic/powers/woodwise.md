**Core power. Free, passive, and deliberately useless in a fight.**

- **She knows the plant and she knows the animal** — what it is, what it does to
  a person, whether it is sick. Her Lore skills cover the woodland she grew up
  in; this covers the rest, at one Difficulty Grade harder in country she does
  not know.
- **She knows whether water is clean**, by looking at it and then by drinking
  it. In a city whose whole crisis is in the water this is the most useful thing
  she owns, and it is the reason to have her in a room.
- **She leaves no trail.** Passive. Tracking her is two Difficulty Grades
  harder, in wild country only — cities keep their own records and a paved
  street remembers nothing either way.
- **Animals are not alarmed by her.** They do not obey her and she cannot ask
  them for anything. Ruhi is a wolf who chose her and stays by preference; that
  is a relationship, not this power.

## Limits

None. It buys nothing that wins a scene.

## Balance note

This replaces three line-items that used to sit in `extras.abilities` doing
nothing, and the druid spell **Animal Friendship**, which is full Classic
Fantasy and not ours. The replacement is deliberately weaker: Animal Friendship
commands, and this does not. If a scene needs an animal to *do* something, that
is Animal Training 52 and a conversation, or it is Ruhi deciding on her own.
