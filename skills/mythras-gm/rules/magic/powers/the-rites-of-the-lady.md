**Core power, carried by the Order of the Lake Lady.** Rites are not spells:
they are not one Action, one Cost and one target, and no amount of Channel makes
one happen faster.

## Free, at will — and only to those who hold it

- **She feels the water.** What has gone *into* the Lady's waters, at distance,
  continuously, in her own body. Not a roll and not a choice; it is how Nerissa
  has lived for two years. It has never once told her what is standing next to
  her, and that limitation is the character.
- **Walking the Lady's water.** On water the Order has consecrated, and nowhere
  else, she may walk on the surface. It is transport and a statement of office,
  and off consecrated water she gets wet like anybody.

## The rites

Each is **one full performance**, not an action, and each requires what its own
line says. The Power Point cost is nominal; the requirement is the rule.

| rite | needs | does |
|---|---|---|
| **The Lady's Washing** — 2 PP | first light, nine of the Order inside the rope, no man armed within it, every fighter out of harness and into a shift | opens a contest on her water |
| **Blessing of the Waters** — 2 PP | the whole Order, the Lady's own water | opens the Tourney |
| **Consecration** — 4 PP | a day and a night, the Order present | makes a water hers |
| **The Asking** — 4 PP | a night, inside the deep pool, alone | one question put to the Lady. **The answer may not come.** Lilura Deepcurrent has fourteen months of it not coming and has told nobody |
| **The Raising** — 8 PP + 1 Experience Roll | the Lady's direct assent, which is an Exhort, and never performed alone | what it sounds like. Three times in Nerissa's lifetime |

## Not hers, and never to be played as hers

**No possession magic of any kind**, by doctrine and by history. The Order has
never had one. That province belongs to `magic/powers/the-binding` and to
`magic/powers/the-wild-line`.

**The Drowning** is not expertise and must never be played as such. It is a
blunt folk rite older than the institution — hold them under, and the Lady keeps
what is not theirs. Nerissa has never done it and watched Lilura do it once, as
a girl, from the back. It is a drowning and a hope and she knows that.

## Limits

**Limited Power**, and it is the whole design. Every rite needs the place, the
people and the time. Away from them **the rites do not happen at all** — not at
a penalty, not at reduced effect. This is what makes a High Priestess formidable
in her temple and an ordinary middle-aged woman on a dredge-boat, and it is the
reason she needs the player characters.

**Activation Cost** on The Asking and The Raising: the caster is *spent*
afterwards — one level of Fatigue for the Asking, three for the Raising, and
the Raising's Experience Roll is permanent.

## Balance note

Nerissa is a Devotion 92 cleric with access to the whole cleric list and a
sensing power nobody else has. **The rites are the thing that keeps her from
solving the campaign**, so the Limited Power limit has to be enforced without
exception. If a rite ever happens on a boat, the character stops working.
