**Power.** Held by both traditions, and every spirit can tell which one is
walking: **the Wild Line walks as a guest, the Binding walks as a hunter.**
Friendly spirits hide from hunters.

This is deliberately **not** Mythras spirit combat. There are no rounds, no
spirit damage and no hit points in the spirit world. A walk is exploration and
conversation; when it turns hostile, one roll settles it.

## The trance — 2 PP

A few minutes of stillness. The walker's body is **helpless, deaf and blind**
while they walk, and should be watched — ideally under Sanctuary
(`magic/powers/the-wild-line`). **Returning is free and at will**, unless
something has snared them (below).

## The spirit world

It is the same place, seen as spirits see it. Holy water shines; a working of
fire is a wound of heat; a seated spirit shows through its host like a lamp
through cloth. **Distance follows meaning, not miles**: the walker reaches what
they are connected to, and the dead are found where they died or where they are
held.

- **Finding** a spirit is **Perception**, helped by Spirit Sight (one grade
  easier). A spirit hiding on purpose gets an opposed **Willpower** roll.
- **Talking** is ordinary social play — **Influence, Customs, Deceit** — against
  the spirit's Willpower where it resists. **A friendly spirit always wants
  something.**

## Hostile spirits: one aim, one roll

A hostile spirit **names one aim**, and the Games Master shows it to the player
**before** the roll. One **opposed Willpower** contest, walker against spirit,
settles it.

| Aim | Spirit wins | Spirit criticals |
|---|---|---|
| **Sever** | The walker is thrown back into their body with **one level of Fatigue**, and cannot walk again that day | It also **knows the walker by name** and can find them, in the spirit world or at their body |
| **Wound** | **1d6** to a hit location of the spirit's choice, ignoring armour, appearing on the body as burns, drowning or whatever the spirit is made of | It also leaves a **mark** Spirit Sight can see, which the spirit can use later to find or open the walker |

**The walker wins:** they choose one — **escape**, **an advantage** (one grade
easier) on their next roll in this walk, or **one question answered** truthfully.
A critical gives two.

**Instead of standing**, before the roll the walker may:

- **Leave.** Always allowed, and free. The spirit knows they ran.
- **Bargain.** An opposed social roll first; success turns the aim aside for a
  price the spirit names.
- **Shelter** under Sanctuary, which works in the spirit world too: an unseated
  spirit cannot reach a walker inside it.

**The walker cannot harm a spirit.** Driving one out of a person stays the work
of Unseat, Exorcism and Draw Forth.

## Snare: a rare trait, never an aim

Ordinary spirits cannot snare. **Snare is a trait a few spirits have**: one
that rules a place, a greater demon a binder called on purpose, the oldest
spirits of a deep wood. A snaring spirit takes the walker and holds them; the
body stays in trance. **This is a scenario seed, never a random encounter** —
getting somebody back is an adventure of its own.

- **The snared walker can break free.** Once per scene of the rescue story, the
  walker rolls opposed **Willpower** against the captor.
- **Help from outside** improves the walker's grade, one step each: a voice they
  love at the body; a rescuer walking in after them; the captor's anchor broken
  in the waking world.
- **A snared walker's body takes no Fatigue** from being held.

The unquiet dead do not snare. A ghost held by its bones is a prisoner, not a
jailer.

## Limits

**Limited Power** — the spirit world only. The walker affects nothing in the
waking world while they walk, and nothing in the waking world can reach them
except through the body.

**Vulnerable body** — whatever happens to the body happens to the walker.

## Balance note

Two Power Points buys a walk, and a whole walk is usually one or two rolls. The
cost is the body left behind, the spirit that now knows your name, and the
Fatigue of being thrown out. Keep the consequence, not the arithmetic, as the
thing that limits it.
