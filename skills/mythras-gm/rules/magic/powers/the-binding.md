**Core power.** The other half of `magic/powers/the-wild-line`, and the two
traditions would be appalled to be named in the same paragraph.

Not spells. Not cast, not Channelled, not Dispelled. **Power Points (= POW).**

## Free, at will

- **Spirit Sight.** As the Wild Line: what is bound and what is loose.
- **Speak with the Bound.**

## Boosts — the sequence

A complete binding is all of these in order, over days, and **every step is a
separate point of failure the party can attack.**

1. **Open the Channel — 2 PP.** Cut a vessel that will not close. An open
   channel is visible to Spirit Sight from across a room and it does not heal.
2. **Draw the seat.** *Not a Boost and not magic* — a worked figure with
   corners, drawn by hand. Anyone can copy one, including somebody who cannot
   do any of the rest of this. Sweeping it is how an unfinished binding is
   stopped by a person with no power at all.
3. **Seat the Bound — 4 PP.** Put the spirit in. The channel closes as it seats.
   Opposed **Willpower** against the host if the host is unwilling and awake.
4. **Anchor — 2 PP each.** One or two objects that hold a piece of the spirit.
   Everything after this is one Difficulty Grade easier while the anchors exist.
   **Take the anchors and the rite gets hard.**
5. **Reinforce the Seat — 1 PP.** Every morning, for as long as the host lasts.
   Miss a morning and the seat begins to slip; miss three and the host's body
   starts to decide the argument.
6. **Draw Forth — 4 PP.** When the host is used up. Requires an anchor and
   somewhere to put it, or the spirit is simply loose in the room.

## Limits

**Limited Power** — spirits and the spirit-touched only, as the Wild Line.

**Fatal Flaw** — a binder must reinforce every seat they hold, every morning.
Held seats are a leash in both directions, and a binder running more than two is
a man who cannot leave town.

## Balance note

The Power Point costs are small on purpose. **This was never meant to be limited
by a budget** — it is limited by time, by the anchors, by the morning
reinforcement, and by the fact that every step leaves evidence. A binder with a
full pool can still be beaten by somebody who finds the anchors, and that is the
shape the campaign wants.

Hanzo has the whole sequence. Santo has the core power and **Open the Channel**
only — he is an apprentice who has cut a channel and does not yet know what to
do with what came through it.
