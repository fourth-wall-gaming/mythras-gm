**Core power.** Heightened Sense, in the Mythras sense, worn by somebody a death
goddess has already counted.

Three things, all passive, none of them costing an action.

- **She does not age.** No mechanical effect and it is not going to acquire one.
  It is a fact about her life and it is worth more at the table than any bonus:
  she has buried everyone.
- **She knows what is behind her.** When attacked from an arc she cannot see,
  she may spend **1 Action Point** to make a **Formidable Perception** roll and
  turn to face. Success means the attack resolves as if she had seen it coming.
- **She feels wrongness.** In the presence of active magic, a bound spirit, or
  an illusion, a **Willpower** roll tells her *that something is wrong and
  roughly where*. It never tells her what it is, and she has no vocabulary for
  it. She is frightened of arcane magic (Fear 54) and this is why.

## Limits

None taken. She does not need them — **Berserk** carries two, which is the
ceiling, and this power is cheap enough not to want paying for.

## Balance note

The third bullet is the one to keep honest. It is a **feeling, not a reading**:
never name the spell, never name the caster, never confirm a suspicion. A
successful roll produces *the hair on her arms*, and the player decides what to
do about it. Played as detection it replaces a whole spell and unbalances every
scene built on not knowing.

## What she also has, which is not a power

**Artful Dodger** is a Classic Fantasy Imperative ability
(`vendor/cfi-srd/rules-en/0003_Classes.md`), taken through her Barbarian
upbringing rather than a class: unburdened and in nothing heavier than light
armour, she may **Evade a melee attack without going prone**, and against a
ranged attack goes prone only on a failure.

Her sheet used to read *"1 extra action for parry/evade"*, which is not that
ability and is not in any of these books. A free Action Point is the strongest
thing in Mythras combat and she should not have one. The CFI version is what
she has.
