**Core power.** Taught nowhere, sold nowhere, and passed hand to hand. Gardwen
has it because somebody who had it decided she should.

Not spells, and this matters mechanically: **the Wild Line is not cast**. It
does not use Channel, it cannot be Counterspelled, it cannot be Dispelled, and
it keeps working when her Magic Points are gone. It runs on **Power Points
(= POW)**, which recover at 1 per minute of rest.

## Free, at will

- **Spirit Sight.** She sees what is bound and what is loose — in a person, an
  object, a room, a stretch of water. Something actively hiding gets an Opposed
  **Willpower** roll against hers.
- **Speak with the Bound.** She can talk to a spirit, seated or not, and it can
  answer. It is under no obligation to be truthful, kind, or sane, and most of
  what is in Purewater's water is none of the three.

## Boosts

- **Sanctuary — 2 PP.** Concentration. She refuses an unseated spirit entry to a
  warded person, doorway or space. It does nothing to a spirit already seated
  and it harms nothing; it simply will not let it in. The standard use is to
  stand over somebody having a binding taken off them.
- **Unseat — 4 PP.** The big one, and the reason the Line is hunted. She takes a
  seated spirit off its host. It is **one full Turn of Concentration** and an
  **Opposed roll: her Willpower against the spirit's**, with the seat's own
  reinforcement counting for the spirit. Broken concentration ends it and the
  Power Points are gone.

  On success the spirit comes out **and is loose in the room**, which is why
  Sanctuary exists and why doing this alone is how people die. On a failure it
  stays and it now knows her.

## Limits

**Limited Power.** It works on spirits and on the spirit-touched. It does
nothing whatever to the living, to the dead who have gone, to worked magic, or
to anything in a book.

## Balance note

At POW 18 she has 18 Power Points, so four Unseats in a day before rest — which
sounds generous until you count what a loose spirit does to the room. **The cost
of Unseat is never the Power Points.** Keep the consequence, not the arithmetic,
as the thing that limits it.

She has one core power and a budget for four, deliberately: she is also a full
cleric, and the Wild Line is meant to be the rare thing she can do, not half of
what she is.
