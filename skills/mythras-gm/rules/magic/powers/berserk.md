**Core power. Free to enter, and it does not ask permission to end.**

Not a class. There is no Berserker class in play — Magda is a Fighter of
Barbarian culture, and this is the thing she does.

## Entering

A free action at the start of her turn, or **no action at all** when she takes a
Serious Wound, sees blood from someone under Love, or is told to stand down by
someone with the standing to be obeyed. In those cases she rolls **Willpower to
stay out**, not to go in. Going in is what her body does.

## While she is over — fourteen rounds

| | |
|---|---|
| Damage Modifier | **+1d2** over her own |
| Weapon size | **one step larger** for attack and parry |
| Damage taken | **halved**, after armour |
| Serious Wounds | **ignored** until it ends — the location still counts, the effect does not land |
| Fatigue | **ignored** until it ends |
| Endurance, Willpower, Brawn | **one grade easier** |
| Parry and Evade | **one grade harder** |

## When it ends

At fourteen rounds, or when there is nothing left standing in reach.

**One level of Fatigue, automatically**, and every wound she ignored arrives at
once — a Serious Wound she fought through applies its full effect now, and she
must make the Endurance roll she did not make at the time.

## Limits

Two, which is what pays for the power.

- **Limited Control.** While she is over, her range of critical failure on
  anything involving violence is **90–00**.
- **Limited Power.** **She cannot retreat and must always engage.** Not a
  penalty to disengaging — she does not disengage. If the only enemy left is
  behind a friend, she goes through the ground the friend is standing on. This
  is the one that has to bite, and at the table it is played as a fact about
  the world rather than a roll she failed.

## Boosts

- **Stay over (2 PP).** Extend by another fourteen rounds, once. The Fatigue at
  the end becomes two levels.
- **Take it with her (2 PP).** On dropping an enemy, immediately engage the next
  one within her Movement as a free action.

## At the table

Never narrate the numbers and never narrate her mind (`TABLE.md §5`). What the
room sees is that she has stopped answering questions.
