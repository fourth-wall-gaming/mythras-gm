A **scroll** is a single casting written out in full by a practitioner fluent enough to do so, for
use by somebody who is not.

- Casting from a scroll requires the **Magic skill**, but **not** knowledge of the spell.
- The roll is **one grade harder** than casting the same spell from knowledge.
- Magic Point cost is unchanged (see `magic/casting`).
- **The scroll is consumed on use regardless of the result.** A failed casting spends the sheet as
  surely as a successful one — which is the single most common way an amateur destroys something
  irreplaceable.

Scrolls are how a practice outlives the practitioner, and how a locked case of them becomes worth
four generations of a family keeping the key. They are also how somebody who cannot read the
language performs a working once: by copying the *seat* by hand and reading the *casting* off the
page.

Anyone with **Read Magic** or the relevant Lore can tell what a scroll does before using it.
Anyone without either is guessing.
