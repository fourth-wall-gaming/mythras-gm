_Touch, Resist Endurance._ The barbed cut. One long line drawn slowly with a hooked instrument, and barbed for one reason only: **so that it will not close behind the caster.**

This is not an injury and it does not behave like one. It is a channel, and it stays open until something is seated in it — the seating is what shuts it. Healing magic will close the flesh and the channel will open it again from the inside within moments, because the wedge is in the working and not in the wound.

An unfinished channel leaves a person **prepared and unoccupied**: a door propped open with nobody in it. Such a vessel is easier to seat than a fresh subject, and anything looking for somewhere to be will find it.

Resisted with Endurance. An unwilling target needs a Grip or surprise.
